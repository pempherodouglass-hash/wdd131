Website Planning Documents

Site Name

Malawi Tourism Guide

The name Malawi Tourism Guide was chosen because the website will provide information about tourist attractions, national parks, cultural sites, and travel opportunities across Malawi.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to promote tourism in Malawi by providing information about tourist destinations, accommodation, travel tips, and activities. Visitors will be able to learn about famous places such as Lake Malawi, Liwonde National Park, and Mount Mulanje.

Scenarios

  • What are the most popular tourist destinations in Malawi?
  • Where can I find accommodation near a tourist attraction?
  • What activities can visitors enjoy at Lake Malawi?
